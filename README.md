# 📌 Project Overview

Customer segmentation enables airlines to understand passenger behavior and develop targeted loyalty strategies. This project applies unsupervised machine learning techniques to group airline customers based on their travel behavior, spending patterns, loyalty, and engagement.

The project compares Agglomerative Clustering and K-Means Clustering to determine the most suitable clustering model before generating actionable business recommendations.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Airlines often treat customers with similar marketing strategies despite differences in travel frequency, spending, and loyalty. Without customer segmentation, marketing campaigns become less effective and customer retention opportunities are missed.

---

# 🎯 Project Goals

- Segment airline customers based on behavioral characteristics.
- Compare Agglomerative Clustering and K-Means Clustering.
- Determine the optimal number of customer clusters.
- Identify behavioral differences between customer segments.
- Generate business recommendations for each segment.

# 📊 Key Insights

- Customer behavior was successfully segmented into two distinct clusters.
- K-Means produced more reliable clusters because it utilized the entire dataset, while Agglomerative Clustering required sampling due to memory limitations.
- High-value customers exhibited higher flight frequency, longer travel distances, greater spending, and stronger loyalty.
- Low-engagement customers traveled less frequently, accumulated fewer loyalty points, and had lower revenue contribution.

# 👥 Customer Segments

### ✈️ High Value Loyal Flyers

- Frequent flyers
- Long-distance travelers
- Long membership tenure
- High revenue contribution
- High loyalty point accumulation
- Active customers

### 🌍 Low Engagement Casual Travelers

- Infrequent flyers
- Short-distance travelers
- Low spending
- Low loyalty point usage
- Recently inactive customers
- New or casual members

- Develop premium loyalty programs.
- Offer business traveler packages.

### Low Engagement Casual Travelers

- Launch personalized promotional campaigns.
- Send reminders before loyalty points expire.
- Offer discounts for repeat flights.
- Encourage reactivation through targeted marketing campaigns.
